## Description

How does a phone that's 5.2 millimeters thick fold in half? We take the iPhone Duo apart in 3D, part by part and layer by layer, down to the atoms: the geared titanium hinge and the sensor that knows its angle, a folding OLED screen made of more than ten layers, the chemistry inside its two batteries, the 2-nanometer transistors in the A20 Pro, the vapor chamber and graphite pipe that carry heat across the fold, the camera sensors, and the Taptic Engine and speakers.
