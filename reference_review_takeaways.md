## 2. iPhone Duo vs a regular iPhone (for a comparison card in chapter 1)

- **Unlock:** Touch ID in the side button, no Face ID.
- **Buttons:** volume on the top edge.
- **Outer screen:** 5.4″ D-shaped, controls on the right; **inner** 7.6″, 1.4 : 1.
- **Front cameras:** a 12 MP outer camera + an under-display camera inside.
- **Chip:** the same A20 Pro as the 18 Pro.
- **Cameras:** the same 48 MP main + ultra wide class as the standard iPhone, no telephoto.
- **Size:** 84 mm wide and 11.3 mm thick closed, ≈ 254 g.
- **Shared with every iPhone:** IP68, MagSafe, USB-C (placed for laptop mode).
